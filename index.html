Welcome to SML3004 Year Abroad Academic Advisor Posts

This video dialogue program asks the placement-status and year-so-far prompts aloud, one at a time, records your spoken reflection, and lets you download the recording as a video file.

Each question will appear on screen as the Academic Advisor voice reads it aloud. Start recording, then let the Academic Advisor voice guide the dialogue. The program will move to the next prompt after your answer, or you can select Answer finished/Pause.

Please note that all urgent or serious personal matters should be reported at once to the Year Abroad team by email or phone. The purpose of this Academic Advisor Post is to discuss more general and not-so-urgent issues.

The recorded video will not be stored on any external platform. It remains on your computer. Before downloading it, you must protect it with a four-digit PIN Number.
